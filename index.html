    Good to know

    FAQ

    What is the best time to visit?

    The drier months, usually around March to May, are great for beaches and rafting. Check the weather before you go.

    How do we reach the region?

    Most travelers fly into Laguindingan Airport near Cagayan de Oro and continue by van and ferry.

    Is it good for beginners?

    Yes. Rafting and park activities have guides and safety briefings, and you can skip any activity.

    What should I bring?

    Swimwear, quick-dry clothes, sunscreen, water shoes, a light rain jacket and a valid ID.
